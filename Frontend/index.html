How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. 01

    Sign in

    Use your school email or registration number. We check you’re on the student register.

  2. 02

    Vote once

    Choose a candidate or abstain for each position, review your ballot, then confirm. It can’t be changed or cast twice.

Designed for trust

A vote you can check,
without giving up privacy.

One ballot per student, a private choice, and a tamper-evident record. When voting closes, everyone sees the same published results.

  • One vote per student

    Eligibility is checked before voting. Repeats and double submits are rejected.

  • Private ballots

    Your identity and choice are kept separate. Nobody can see who you chose.

  • Tamper-evident record

    Each accepted ballot has a verifiable reference. Quiet edits do not go unnoticed.

  • Receipt verification

    Check your receipt anytime. It proves counting without revealing your choice.

  • Results at close

    Totals are published after voting ends, using the same result for everyone.

Your choice stays private.

Your receipt confirms that a ballot was counted. It never shows which candidate you selected.
